# Unit 3 — Reflection [HUMAN]

## Prompt (300–500 words)

After building your 5-prompt library (exercise 3.1):

1. Which of the unit's techniques gave you the clearest improvement? Show a before and after (you can quote short fragments).
2. Was there a technique that didn't work the way you expected? Come up with a hypothesis for why.
3. Think about your typical week: how many hours do you estimate your library could save you? Explain how you made the estimate and how reliable it is.

## Rubric (12 points)

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Proficient (2) | Developing (1) | Insufficient (0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before/after evidence | Specific fragments that show the improvement | Partial evidence | A claim with no evidence | Missing |
| Analysis of what didn't work | A reasoned hypothesis connected to the guide | A hypothesis with no connection | A mention with no analysis | Missing |
| Savings estimate | Explicit method and acknowledged limits | An estimate with a weak method | A number with no method | Missing |
| Clarity | Organized, precise text | Somewhat disorganized | Hard to follow | Illegible or off topic |

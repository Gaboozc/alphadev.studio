# Unit 4 — Graded Exercises

Solutions in `../claves/u4-soluciones-ejercicios.md`.

## Level 1

**1.1 [AUTO]** A tool costs $30/month and saves you 4 hours a month. You value your hour at $25. Calculate: a) the value of the time saved; b) the value/cost ratio; c) the ROI as a percentage.

**1.2 [AUTO]** Put the stages of a monthly report workflow in order: (a) the person verifies numbers and adds context; (b) the data is exported; (c) AI proposes findings, citing numbers; (d) it's sent to the client; (e) the data is anonymized.

**1.3 [AUTO]** Which of these tasks should NOT be left without human review? a) Generating 20 headline ideas. b) Answering a client's formal complaint about a charge. c) Proposing an article outline. d) Rephrasing an internal paragraph.

## Level 2

**2.1 [HUMAN]** Pick a weekly task from your work or studies. Measure how long it currently takes (at least 3 times). Design an AI workflow as a table (stage, who, detail, checkpoint). Run it 3 times and measure. Submit the table, the measurements and the net savings (including review).

**2.2 [HUMAN]** Apply the verification protocol (section 4) to a 300+ word AI-generated text on a topic in your field with at least 5 specific facts. Submit a table: fact / source / correct or not / correction.

## Level 3

**3.1 [HUMAN]** An honest AI map (adapted from the original project ia-1-proj-basico): for a real or fictional business with at least 5 services or areas, say where AI helps (with which tool, at which stage, what approximate percentage of the work, and why you estimate that percentage) and where it doesn't (and why). Close with 150 words on how the value proposition changes.

**3.2 [HUMAN]** Write a one-page AI use policy for a 5-person team: what can and can't be pasted in, how things are verified, how clients are informed, which tools are approved and who decides.

# Unit 4 — Reflection [HUMAN]

## Prompt (300–500 words)

Imagine a client or your boss asks you, "Why should I pay you the same if you're using AI now?" Write your reasoned answer:

1. Which part of your work AI does and which part is still yours (with examples from your workflow in exercise 2.1).
2. Which risks you take on and how you control them.
3. Whether you'd change how you charge (by the hour, by project, by outcome) and why.
4. Which ethical limit you set for yourself, even if the client doesn't ask for it.

## Rubric (15 points)

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Proficient (2) | Developing (1) | Insufficient (0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Division of labor | Distinguishes, with specific examples, what AI does and what human judgment does | A general distinction with some example | A vague distinction | Missing |
| Risks and controls | Specific risks with concrete controls | Risks without clear controls | A generic mention | Missing |
| Pricing model | A reasoned position with pros and cons | A position with a partial argument | A position with no argument | Missing |
| Ethical limit | A specific, justified limit | A general limit | A vague statement of commitment | Missing |
| Argumentation | Persuasive, organized, no contradictions | Organized with some weakness | Disorganized | Incoherent |

# Final Project — Your Personal AI Working System

**Weeks 9–10 · Weight: 35% · Replaces and merges:** ia-1-proj-basico (AI map) and prodai-1-proj-basico (optimize 3 prompts), which in the original material were two nearly identical projects in two different branches.

## Brief

You'll design, test and document your own AI working system for a real setting: your job, your freelance practice, your studies or a business you know well (with permission if you use real data). The goal is to show that you know how to turn AI into a reliable, measurable process, not just write good prompts.

## Deliverables

1. **Diagnosis (1–2 pages).** Context, frequent tasks and the measured time of at least 3 tasks without AI (at least 3 measurements each).
2. **Honest AI map.** At least 5 areas or tasks: where AI helps (tool, stage, estimated percentage with a justification) and where it doesn't (with a reason).
3. **Prompt library (5 entries)** with every field from U3. At least 3 must come from prompts you already used, with the original version, the optimized one and the iteration table (one change per version). At least 2 must use examples (*few-shot*) and at least 1 must produce structured output.
4. **A complete workflow** for one of the tasks: a table of stages, owners and checkpoints; run at least 3 times with measurements; net savings, and ROI calculated correctly if there are paid tools.
5. **Verification log:** a deliverable produced with the workflow, with the table of verified facts.
6. **Personal use policy** (1 page max): what you don't paste in, how you verify, how you disclose.
7. **Final analysis (500–700 words):** what worked, what didn't, which change produced the biggest improvement and what you'd do differently. AI-use disclosure.

## How it's graded

### Automatic part (checklist) — 20% of the project [AUTO]

Checks the presence and format of: a diagnosis with ≥ 3 tasks × ≥ 3 measurements; a map with ≥ 5 areas; 5 entries with every field; ≥ 2 with examples; ≥ 1 with structured output (valid JSON or table); a workflow with ≥ 3 measurements; ROI with the formula (benefit − cost)/cost; a policy of ≤ 1 page; an analysis of 500 to 700 words; an AI-use disclosure. Each missing item deducts proportionally.

### Human part (rubric) — 80% of the project [HUMAN]

| Criterion (weight) | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Sufficient (2) | Insufficient (0–1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prompt quality (20%) | Complete, clear, with demonstrated and documented improvements | Complete, with partial improvements | Some weak component, or improvements without evidence | Generic prompts |
| Workflow design (20%) | Clear stages, human checkpoints well placed and justified | A correct workflow with some misplaced checkpoint | An incomplete workflow | No real workflow |
| Measurement and return (15%) | Repeated measurements, honest net savings, correct ROI | Measurements with some weakness | Single measurements, or review not included | No measurement |
| Verification (15%) | Every claim verified with a primary source | Most verified | Superficial verification | No verification |
| Honest map and ethics (15%) | Includes real limits; a concrete, workable policy | Limits present, general policy | Weak limits | Overestimates AI, no policy |
| Analysis and reflection (15%) | Evidence-based conclusions, self-critical | Reasonable conclusions | Descriptive | Missing or generic |

**Passing the project:** 60%. The grader may request a 10-minute oral defense if there are doubts about authorship.

# Unit 2 — Reflection [HUMAN]

## Prompt (300–500 words)

Look at the last five prompts you wrote to an AI before this course (find them in your history). Choose two.

1. For each one, identify which components were missing and what effect that had on the answer you got.
2. Rewrite one of them using the unit's template.
3. Reflect: which component do you tend to forget most, and why do you think that happens? What does that tell you about how you communicate requests to people, not just to AI?

## Rubric (12 points)

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Proficient (2) | Developing (1) | Insufficient (0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Analysis of real prompts | Two real prompts, missing components correctly identified and connected to the result | Two prompts with partial analysis | One prompt, or shallow analysis | No real prompts |
| Rewrite | A complete, coherent, usable rewritten prompt | A rewrite with one or two weak components | An incomplete rewrite | Missing |
| Self-diagnosis | Identifies a personal pattern with a plausible cause | Identifies the pattern without a cause | A vague mention | Missing |
| Transfer | Connects concretely to communicating with people | A general connection | A superficial mention | Missing |

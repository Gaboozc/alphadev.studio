# Unit 2 — Quiz

## Part A — Multiple choice

1. Why does a vague prompt tend to produce generic text?
   a) Because the model deliberately penalizes short prompts.
   b) Because the model tries less hard when the request is very brief.
   c) Because short prompts are allowed fewer tokens.
   d) Because it leaves many possible continuations and the most common one wins.

2. What does assigning a role to the model really contribute?
   a) Direction for tone, vocabulary and approach.
   b) Faster, more precise answers.
   c) Verified expert knowledge of the field.
   d) Access to specialized industry sources.

3. "It'll be read by first-semester students with no prior knowledge" is an example of:
   a) role  b) audience  c) context  d) format

4. Which is the best constraint?
   a) "Short and easy to read, no beating around the bush."
   b) "100 words max, in three paragraphs."
   c) "Don't make it too long or too short."
   d) "Don't be boring, and get to the point."

5. When iterating on a prompt, the recommended practice is:
   a) Make it longer with more detail until it improves.
   b) Try different models until one works.
   c) Rewrite it completely on every new iteration.
   d) Change one component at a time and note the effect.

6. The answer includes a discount that doesn't exist. The component that most likely failed is:
   a) Role: nobody defined who's speaking or their experience.  b) Audience: nobody said who it's for.  c) Context and constraints: no data and no "don't make things up."  d) Format: no output structure was requested.

7. Why specify the output format?
   a) To always spend fewer tokens on the answer.
   b) Because without a format the model can't answer.
   c) To be able to compare, reuse and verify the answer.
   d) For looks: a tidy answer looks better.

8. Which of these evaluation criteria can be verified without human judgment?
   a) "It sounds like our usual brand."
   b) "The tone is warm and very friendly."
   c) "It's persuasive for the client."
   d) "The subject line is 45 characters max."

9. A 400-word prompt with contradictory information versus a clear 80-word one:
   a) The short, clear one usually gives a better result.
   b) The long one wins, because it provides much more context.
   c) It depends only on the temperature chosen.
   d) They're the same if the model is powerful.

10. Using AI to list "my customers' most common objections" is useful as:
    a) A substitute for customer interviews.
    b) A citable source in a client report.
    c) A brainstorm that has to be validated with customers.
    d) Market data already confirmed by the model.

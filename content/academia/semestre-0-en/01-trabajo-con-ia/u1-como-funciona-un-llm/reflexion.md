# Unit 1 — Reflection (reviewed with a rubric)

## Prompt (300–500 words)

Think of a time, real or recent, when you used AI (or saw someone use it) and the result surprised you, for better or worse. Using what you learned in this unit:

1. Describe the situation: what was asked and what it answered.
2. Explain **why** it happened, using at least two concepts from the unit (tokens, prediction, cutoff date, temperature, context window, hallucination).
3. What would you do differently now? Be specific: write how you'd change the request or the process.
4. What changed in how you trust AI after this unit?

If you used AI to help you write, say so in one line at the end.

## Rubric (12 points)

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Proficient (2) | Developing (1) | Insufficient (0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Description of the case | A specific case, with a clear request and answer | A specific case with some missing detail | A vague or generic case | No case |
| Use of concepts | Two or more concepts used precisely to explain causes | Two concepts, one used imprecisely | One concept, or concepts mentioned without connecting them to the case | No concepts, or conceptual errors |
| Proposed improvement | A specific, verifiable change (a rewritten prompt or a new process step) | A specific but underdeveloped change | A general intention ("I'd do it better") | No proposal |
| Metacognition | Honest reflection on their level of trust and how they'll adjust it | Reflection present but shallow | A generic sentence | Missing |

**Notes for the grader:** value conceptual precision over length. Penalize (criterion 2 = 0) statements like "the AI looked it up in its database." If there's no AI-use disclosure and the text is obviously generated, apply the course policy.

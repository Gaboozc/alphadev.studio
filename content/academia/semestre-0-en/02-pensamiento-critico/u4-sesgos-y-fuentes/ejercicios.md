# Unit 4 — Tiered Exercises

Solutions in `../claves/u4-soluciones-ejercicios.md`.

## Level 1 (auto-graded)

**1.1** Name the bias:
a) "We're sticking with the vendor because we already paid for the integration, even though it breaks every week."
b) "After seeing three news stories about hacks, the client believes their site will be attacked tomorrow."
c) "The client said 'around $300,' and our proposal came out at $350."
d) "I read 10 stories of freelancers who got rich from their course; I'm going to make one."
e) "I only checked the positive comments about the launch because I knew it had gone well."

**1.2** Match each letter of SIFT to its action: S, I, F, T → (1) trace back to the original; (2) look for other sources; (3) stop; (4) investigate who's publishing.

**1.3** Calculation: a pen and a notebook cost $22 in total; the notebook costs $20 more than the pen. How much does the pen cost?

## Level 2 (reviewed with a rubric)

**2.1** Pick a digital marketing statistic you've seen repeated (e.g., in a presentation or blog). Apply SIFT and document each step. Conclusion: can it be used? How should it be cited?

**2.2** Design process countermeasures (not "be careful") for three biases in a team that estimates timelines for web projects.

## Level 3 (reviewed with a rubric)

**3.1** Ask an AI assistant for an answer with at least five verifiable claims (figures, dates, sources) on a topic from your field. Apply the protocol from section 4 and turn in a table: claim · source consulted · result (correct / incorrect / unverifiable) · action.

**3.2** Evaluate two sources on the same topic with CRAAP (one commercial and one academic or institutional) and argue which one you'd use in a report for a client (300 words).

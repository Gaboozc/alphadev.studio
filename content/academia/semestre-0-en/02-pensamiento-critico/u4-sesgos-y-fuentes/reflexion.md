# Unit 4 — Reflection [HUMAN]

## Prompt (300–500 words)
For one week, write down each time you catch yourself in one of the unit's biases (at least three episodes). Then write:
1. The episodes: situation, bias, what you did.
2. What pattern do you see? In which situations are you most vulnerable?
3. One process countermeasure you'll adopt, and how you'll know whether it works.

## Rubric (12 points)
| Criterion | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Episodes | Three or more, concrete and well classified | Three, with some questionable classification | One or two | None |
| Pattern | Identifies conditions (fatigue, pressure, self-interest) | General pattern | No pattern | Missing |
| Countermeasure | Process-based, concrete and measurable | Concrete, not measurable | "Be careful" | Missing |
| Honesty | Genuine self-critique | Partial | Defensive | Missing |

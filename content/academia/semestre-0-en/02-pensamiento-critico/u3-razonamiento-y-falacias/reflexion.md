# Unit 3 — Reflection (reviewed with a rubric)

## Prompt (300–500 words)
Think of a discussion (at work, at school or in your personal life) where you used a weak or fallacious argument, even unintentionally.
1. Reconstruct your argument: conclusion, premises, assumptions.
2. Identify the error or fallacy.
3. What effect did it have on the conversation or the decision?
4. Rewrite the argument the way you'd make it today.

## Rubric (12 points)
| Criterion | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reconstruction | Complete, with implicit assumptions | No assumptions | Confusing | Missing |
| Identifying the error | Named and explained precisely | Named, not explained | Wrong | Missing |
| Analysis of the effect | Connects the error to concrete consequences | General | Superficial | Missing |
| Rewrite and self-critique | Improved argument and honesty about their own bias | Partial improvement | No real improvement | Missing |

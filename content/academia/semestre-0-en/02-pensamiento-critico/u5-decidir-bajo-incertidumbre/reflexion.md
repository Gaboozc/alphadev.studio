# Unit 5 — Reflection (reviewed with a rubric)

## Prompt (300–500 words)
Think of an important decision you made at least six months ago.
1. Reconstruct what you knew then, what options you had and what you expected.
2. Separate decision quality from outcome: was it a good decision with bad luck, a bad decision with good luck, or some other combination?
3. Which tool from this unit would have changed your process, and how?

## Rubric (12 points)
| Criterion | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reconstruction | Information, options and expectations as they were then, without hindsight bias | Partial | Narrates only the outcome | Missing |
| Decision vs. outcome | Clear, well-argued separation | Separation without argument | Confuses the two | Missing |
| Applying the tools | A specific tool applied to the case | General mention | Superficial | Missing |
| Learning | A concrete process change for the future | General | Vague | Missing |

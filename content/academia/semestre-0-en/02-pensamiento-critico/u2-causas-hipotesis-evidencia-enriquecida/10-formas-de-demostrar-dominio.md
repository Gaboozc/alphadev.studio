# 10 · Ways to Demonstrate Mastery

**Id:** `s0-c2-u2-dominio` · **Format:** menu of evidence options with a shared rubric · **Assessment:** [HUMAN]/[MIXED] · **Rule:** each student chooses **one** pathway. All are assessed with the **same rubric** against the **same objectives**.

**Why:**
- UDL calls for multiple means of action and expression, that is, several ways to show what you've learned [VERIFIED: CAST UDL Guidelines, https://udlguidelines.cast.org/].
- Autonomy sustains motivation [VERIFIED: Ryan and Deci 2000, https://doi.org/10.1037//0003-066x.55.1.68].
- **What doesn't change with the pathway** is the **level of rigor** [INFERENCE: the design principle].

**What it replaces:** the chosen pathway takes the place of the original unit's level 3 exercises (3.1 and 3.2) within the 20% for exercises. The quiz stays the same for everyone [INFERENCE: proposal; Gabe approves it].

## 2. Shared rubric (4 criteria × 4 levels)

The same objectives as the original guide: falsifiable hypotheses, root cause, correlation vs. causation, test design and quality of evidence.

| Criterion | 4 · Outstanding | 3 · Proficient | 2 · Developing | 1 · Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Hypotheses and alternatives** | ≥ 3 falsifiable hypotheses or alternatives specific to the case, including reverse causation, confounding, selection or measurement where they apply | ≥ 3, some generic | 1–2, or hard to falsify | A single explanation |
| **2. Use of evidence** | Links each alternative to the data that distinguishes it, uses a comparison and rates the evidence with the hierarchy | Uses data to rule out most | Mentions data without connecting it to the alternatives | Opinion without data |
| **3. Cause and conclusion** | Reaches a systemic cause or a conclusion proportional to the evidence; states the uncertainty | Reasonable conclusion, with a leap | Conclusion that exceeds the evidence | Conclusion unrelated to the analysis |
| **4. Next test** | Feasible test with assignment, metric, duration and refutation criterion | Missing one element | Vague | None |

**Format adjustments** (the criteria don't change):
- **Pathway B:** spoken delivery isn't scored separately. If the audio can't be understood, the transcript is requested.
- **Pathway D:** criterion 2 is assessed on the written summary and the observation rubric.
- Passing is ≥ 10/16 [INFERENCE: the project's 60% threshold, rounded].

## 3. AI policy (shared)

- The analysis, hypotheses and test must be yours. For pathways B and D, the instructor may ask for a short conversation about your submission to check this [INFERENCE: consistent with `plan-revision.md`, section 5].

## 4. Strategy question for the unit reflection

Add this question to the original reflection (`../u2-causas-hipotesis-evidencia/reflexion.md`). It doesn't change its 300–500-word length or its rubric:

> **About how you learned:**
> - Which pathways in this unit did you use (guide, podcast, map, examples, flashcards, case, challenge, role-play)?
> - In which did you feel you understood the most, and in which did you check that you really knew it?
> - Did they match? Compare your flashcard prediction with your actual result.
> - What would you change about how you study U3?

**Why this question:** the feeling of mastery and actual learning don't always match [VERIFIED: Soderstrom and Bjork, https://bjorklab.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2016/07/Soderstrom_Bjork_Learning_versus_Performance.pdf]. Teaching people to monitor their own learning improves outcomes [VERIFIED: EEF, https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/metacognition].

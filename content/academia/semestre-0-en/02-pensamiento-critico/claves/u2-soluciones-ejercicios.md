# Unit 2 — Solutions

**1.1** **b and d**. (a and c don't say what would disprove them.)
**1.2** Mainly **reverse causation** (stores that sell more hire more) and/or **confounding** (store size or location causes both). Either is accepted if justified; the best answer mentions both.
**1.3** **b → d → a → c**.

**2.1 (reference)** Valid hypotheses: a change in the platform's targeting or algorithm; more competition in the auction (seasonality); ad fatigue (the same creatives for weeks); a conversion tracking failure (cost "rises" because conversions aren't being counted); a change to the landing page. Prioritize the cheap, likely ones first (tracking failure, landing page change).

**2.2 (reference)** E.g.: wrong amount → an old rate was used → the rate was copied by hand from a spreadsheet → the spreadsheet wasn't updated after the price change → there's no single source of prices connected to billing. Action: a single source of prices and automatic validation before invoices go out.

**2.3 (reference)** People (they don't understand why the data is requested; distrust), Processes (too many steps; mandatory email verification before seeing any value), Technology (bugs on Android; slowness), Data (unnecessary data requested; poor funnel measurement), Environment (they arrive from ads with a different promise). Graded on whether these are causes and not solutions.

**3.1 (reference)** Alternatives: seasonality; other simultaneous actions (referrals, campaigns); a change in how requests are logged; chance, given small volume. Test: ask on the form how they heard about the agency, use links with tracking parameters, compare with the same period last year, pause or vary posting frequency and observe.

**3.2 (reference)** Directional hypothesis; primary metric (form submission rate or calls booked); random 50/50 assignment; duration set in advance (e.g., until a calculated sample size or at least two full weeks to cover every day of the week); decision criterion; threats (traffic changes during the test, peeking at results and stopping early, implementation errors).

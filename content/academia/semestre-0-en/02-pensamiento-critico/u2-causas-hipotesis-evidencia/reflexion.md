# Unit 2 — Reflection [HUMAN]

## Prompt (300–500 words)
Find a recent causal claim around you (at work, in industry news, on social media): "X caused Y."
1. Quote the claim and its source.
2. Evaluate the evidence: what type is it, according to the hierarchy in section 5?
3. Propose at least two alternative explanations.
4. What data would you need to believe it? Did you change your mind during the analysis?

## Rubric (12 points)
| Criterion | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claim and source | Direct quote with a verifiable source | Paraphrase with source | No clear source | Missing |
| Evaluation of the evidence | Classified and justified | Classified, not justified | Incorrect | Missing |
| Alternatives | Two or more, plausible and specific | Two generic ones | One | None |
| Data needed and intellectual honesty | Concrete data point and reflection on their own change of mind | Concrete data point | Vague | Missing |

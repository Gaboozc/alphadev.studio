# Unit 1 — Quiz

Key in `../claves/u1-clave-cuestionario.md`. **Part A [AUTO]** · **Part B [HUMAN]**

1. According to the unit, "sales dropped" is:
   a) a solution b) a problem c) a cause d) a symptom
2. What's the biggest risk of accepting the client's proposed solution without analyzing it?
   a) Treating a symptom instead of its real cause. b) Taking longer to deliver the project. c) None: the client knows their business. d) Charging less than the work is worth.
3. A problem statement should NOT include:
   a) the measured current situation b) the constraints c) the success criterion d) the chosen solution
4. The question "where does the problem not happen?" helps you:
   a) Cut the budget and scope of the analysis. b) Find differences that point to the cause. c) Reassure the client with good news. d) Close the case as soon as possible and move on.
5. A MECE breakdown is:
   a) Very detailed, with many levels. b) Always three branches per level. c) Free of overlaps and gaps. d) Sorted alphabetically.
6. Revenue flat, traffic +20%, average order value unchanged. What happened to conversion?
   a) It went up b) It went down c) No change d) There's no way to know
7. The Pólya step most often skipped in real work is usually:
   a) Understanding the problem b) Devising a plan c) Carrying out the plan d) Looking back
8. "What changed when the problem started?" mainly helps you:
   a) Generate cause hypotheses b) Define the project scope c) Measure the financial impact d) Find who's to blame
9. A good success criterion is:
   a) "Improve the website so it loads much faster" b) "Do it fast, before the quarter ends" c) "Load < 2.5 s on 75% of visits, for 14 days" d) "The client is happy with the new website"
10. "Working backward" means:
    a) Going back to the project's previous version b) Copying the structure of a similar project c) Starting from the desired result and deducing what comes before d) Reviewing earlier work in reverse order

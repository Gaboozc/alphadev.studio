# Unit 1 — Tiered Exercises

Solutions in `../claves/u1-soluciones-ejercicios.md`.

## Level 1

**1.1 [AUTO]** Classify each sentence as a symptom (S), problem (P), cause (C) or solution (Sol):
a) "Campaign emails have been landing in the spam folder since we switched providers."
b) "People are opening fewer emails."
c) "The open rate fell from 32% to 11%, and we need to get back to at least 25%."
d) "Set up domain authentication with the new provider."

**1.2 [AUTO]** Which breakdown of "customers who cancel their subscription" is MECE?
a) Because of price / because of a bad experience / young people.
b) Cancel in the first month / between months 2 and 12 / after month 12.
c) Through the app / to the competition / by email.
d) Angry customers / customers in Mexico.

**1.3 [AUTO]** In the identity revenue = traffic × conversion × average order value, traffic rose 10%, average order value didn't change, and revenue fell 12%. Which factor changed, and in which direction?

## Level 2

**2.1 [HUMAN]** Turn this complaint into a complete statement (all six elements): "Nobody uses the new reporting feature we launched." Make up realistic data and point out which data you'd need to confirm.

**2.2 [HUMAN]** Write the 7 questions you'd ask a client who says: "We need to redesign the website because it doesn't bring in customers."

**2.3 [HUMAN]** Build a three-level MECE issue tree for "customer support response time has doubled."

## Level 3

**3.1 [HUMAN]** Case. A startup asks for "a mobile app because users complain about the website." On review, you find that 80% of complaints mention they can't recover their password. Write a 150–200-word email to the client that reframes the problem without dismissing their proposal.

**3.2 [HUMAN]** Apply Pólya's four steps to a real problem from your work or studies. Document each step and which heuristic you used in step 2.

# Unit 1 — Reflection (reviewed with a rubric)

## Prompt (300–500 words)
Think of a project (work, school or personal) where you solved the wrong problem or jumped to a solution too soon.
1. What was the symptom, what did you think the problem was, and what did it turn out to be?
2. Which question from section 3 would have saved you time?
3. Rewrite the problem statement the way it should have been.
4. What pushes you to jump to solutions (pressure, enthusiasm, client expectations)? What will you do differently?

## Rubric (12 points)
| Criterion | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Case and symptom/problem distinction | Clear and precise | Clear, with inaccuracies | Confusing | Missing |
| Key question | Relevant and justified | Relevant, not justified | Barely relevant | Missing |
| Rewritten statement | All six elements | 4–5 elements | Fewer than 4 | Missing |
| Self-awareness | Own pattern and a concrete action | Pattern, no action | Vague | Missing |

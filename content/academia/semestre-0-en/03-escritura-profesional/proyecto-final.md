# Final Project — Communication Kit

**Week 10 · 35% of the grade · Mixed assessment**

*This project replaces and merges the original projects "Rewrite the value proposition" (copy-1-proj-basico) and "Complete sales email" (copy-1-proj-inter), and adds microcopy (UX writing) and a proposal.*

## Brief

Choose a real business (your own, a client's, or a local one with permission) or a well-defined fictional one. Create its **communication kit**: the key texts it would need to introduce itself, sell and support its users.

If it's fictional, submit a 150-word profile (what it sells, to whom, differentiator, approximate prices). **Every invented fact, number or testimonial must be labeled [FICTIONAL].** For a real business, only true data.

## Deliverables

**Part 1 — Value proposition and homepage (U1–U2)**
- The original value proposition (or a competitor's) with an analysis.
- 3 versions with different angles; the chosen one, justified.
- Full copy for the top of the homepage: headline, subheadline, 3 benefits (each with its supporting feature), social proof and a call to action. Say which structure (AIDA/PAS/FAB/BAB) and which principles you're using.

**Part 2 — Microcopy for a flow (U3)**
- A 4–6 screen flow (sign-up, booking, purchase or similar) with all the text: titles, labels, helper text, buttons, 2 error messages, 1 empty state, a confirmation.
- A minimal style guide (voice, register, glossary of 5+ terms, rules, patterns).

**Part 3 — Proposal (U4)**
- A proposal for a specific client of the business (or for the business as an agency's client), with an executive summary and all 7 parts, pricing options and correct calculations (with taxes if applicable). 800–1,500 words.

**Part 4 — Sales email and follow-up (U4)**
- 3 subject line + preview text variations.
- A complete sales email and a mobile screenshot (approx. 375 px).
- A follow-up email.

**Part 5 — Process memo (300–500 words)**
- Main decisions, how you used AI (key prompts) and what you verified or changed, ethical limits you applied.

## Assessment

### Automatic part — checklist (20%)
Yes/no, 2 points each:
1. All five parts present with their headings.
2. Part 1: 3 value proposition versions + the chosen one.
3. Part 1: headline, subheadline, 3 benefits, social proof and CTA present.
4. Part 2: ≥ 4 screens, ≥ 2 errors, ≥ 1 empty state.
5. Part 2: style guide with a glossary of ≥ 5 terms.
6. Part 3: all 7 parts + executive summary.
7. Part 3: length 800–1,500 words; pricing totals arithmetically correct.
8. Part 4: 3 subject lines + preview text; mobile screenshot attached.
9. Part 4: follow-up email present.
10. Invented data labeled [FICTIONAL] (or a statement that all data is real).

### Human part — rubric (80%)

| Criterion (weight) | Excellent (4) | Proficient (3) | Developing (2) | Insufficient (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Clarity (20%)** | Every text applies U1: a clear reader, essentials first, clean sentences, no filler | Clear with minor lapses | Several texts confusing or padded | Hard to understand |
| **Persuasion and value proposition (20%)** | A specific, uncopyable proposition; true benefits; well-chosen structure and principles | A good proposition, somewhat generic | Centered on the company or on features | Generic, no benefit |
| **UX writing (20%)** | Clear, useful microcopy consistent with the guide; actionable errors; adapted tone | Good, with minor inconsistencies | Ambiguous buttons or unhelpful errors | Doesn't apply the principles |
| **Proposal and emails (25%)** | Starts with the client; explicit scope; price with ethical context; emails with effective subject lines, CTA and follow-up | Complete with minor weaknesses | Ambiguous scope or price; weak CTA | Incomplete |
| **Ethics and verification (15%)** | No misleading data; fictional items labeled; the memo shows AI verification and clear ethical limits | Correct ethics, superficial memo | Some questionable, unlabeled claim | Invented data presented as real (penalizes the whole criterion) |

**Grade** = checklist (20%) + rubric weighted to 80%. At least 60% is required to pass the course.

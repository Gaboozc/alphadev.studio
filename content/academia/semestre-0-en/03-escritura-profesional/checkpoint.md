## Part B — Applied case: a physical therapy practice [fictional case] [HUMAN]

"North Physio," a physical therapy practice with two locations, asked you for a quote to rebuild its website and online booking system. The owner is short on time and reads email on her phone. Today they lose 30% of appointments because patients can't figure out how to book.

**What you need to submit** (250–350 words in total, 30–40 min):

1. Write the email that goes with the proposal: a subject line and 150 words max, using the inverted pyramid and a single call to action.
2. Write the service's value proposition in one specific, verifiable sentence.
3. Write the "time slot not available" error message and the "you don't have any appointments yet" empty state for their app.
4. Add a one-line AI-use disclosure, if you used it.

**AI policy:** you may use AI to generate ideas, not to write the analysis. If you used it, say so in one line at the end.

### Rubric (12 points; visible from the start)

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Proficient (2) | Developing (1) | Insufficient (0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Email | Informative subject line, request in the first line, ≤ 150 words and a single call to action | Meets three of the four | Meets one or two | Missing |
| Value proposition | Specific, centered on the benefit (recovered appointments) and verifiable | Specific but centered on features | Generic and copyable | Missing |
| Microcopy | An error with what happened and what to do, no blame; an empty state that orients and offers the action | One of the two done well | Both vague or blaming | Missing |
| Clarity and ethics | Short sentences, active voice, no unverifiable promises, AI use disclosed | A somewhat unclear sentence or a vague promise | Several unsupported promises | Missing |

Part B passes with ≥ 7/12 [INFERENCE].

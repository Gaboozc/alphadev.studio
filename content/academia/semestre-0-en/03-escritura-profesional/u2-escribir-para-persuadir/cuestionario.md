# Unit 2 — Quiz

Key in `../claves/u2-clave-cuestionario.md`. **Part A [AUTO]** · **Part B [HUMAN]**

1. The goal of copywriting is to:
   a) Inform neutrally. b) Prompt a specific action. c) Rank in search engines. d) Use pretty, catchy words.
2. A benefit describes:
   a) What the customer gets or avoids. b) The technology the product uses. c) What the product has or includes. d) The price and payment options.
3. A weak value proposition is one that:
   a) A competitor could copy without changing anything. b) Clearly names the audience it's for. c) Mentions an outcome the customer gets. d) Is specific and can be verified with data.
4. How many principles did Cialdini describe in *Influence* (1984)?
   a) 5 b) 6 c) 7 d) 10
5. The principle of unity was introduced in:
   a) None of his books. b) *Influence* (1984). c) A 1974 paper. d) *Pre-Suasion* (2016).
6. A "deal ends in 10 minutes" timer that resets every time you visit is:
   a) Legitimate, ethical scarcity. b) The seller's authority. c) Fake scarcity: manipulation. d) Reciprocity with the customer.
7. PAS fits best when:
   a) The reader recognizes an urgent problem. b) There's a spec sheet to compare. c) You want to tell a success story. d) The reader doesn't know about the problem yet.
8. FAB is especially useful for:
   a) Emotional brand copy. b) Error messages in an app. c) Audiences comparing with data. d) Sales email subject lines.
9. The attribution of AIDA to E. St. Elmo Lewis:
   a) Is fully proven by documents. b) Is common, but debated by historians. c) Is false, according to period documents. d) Doesn't appear in any published source.
10. A number in a testimonial must:
    a) Be in bold to stand out. b) Be high, to impress. c) Be round, to be memorable. d) Be real and verifiable.

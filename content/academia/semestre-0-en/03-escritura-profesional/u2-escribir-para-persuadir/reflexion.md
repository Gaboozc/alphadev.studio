# Unit 2 — Reflection (reviewed with a rubric)

## Prompt (300–500 words)
Think back to a purchase (or subscription) where some copy or an offer convinced you.
1. Which persuasion principles or structures do you recognize now?
2. Was it ethical persuasion or manipulation? Apply the test from section 4 ("would you be comfortable if you knew the technique?").
3. How did you feel after the purchase?
4. What ethical limits do you set for your own copy?

## Rubric (12 points)
| Criterion | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Identifying techniques | Principles and structure identified, with quotes from the copy | Identified without quotes | Errors | Missing |
| Ethical judgment | Argued with the test from section 4 | A judgment without argument | Superficial | Missing |
| Personal experience | Connects the technique to its effect on them | General | Vague | Missing |
| Personal limits | Specific and workable | General | Vague | Missing |

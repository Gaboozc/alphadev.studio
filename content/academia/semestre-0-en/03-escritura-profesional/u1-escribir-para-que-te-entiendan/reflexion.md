# Unit 1 — Reflection (reviewed with a rubric)

## Prompt (300–500 words)
Dig up a professional or academic text you wrote a while ago (an email, a report, a paper).
1. Analyze it with the checklist from section 6: which problems do you find?
2. Were you thinking about the reader when you wrote it? How can you tell?
3. Rewrite a passage (100–150 words) and explain what you changed.
4. Which writing habit are you going to change?

## Rubric (12 points)
| Criterion | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | Specific problems quoted and classified | General problems | Superficial | Missing |
| Reader's perspective | Honestly analyzes reader, purpose and action | Partial | A vague mention | Missing |
| Rewrite | A clear, justified improvement | An improvement without justification | Minimal changes | Missing |
| Habit | Specific and workable | General | Vague | Missing |

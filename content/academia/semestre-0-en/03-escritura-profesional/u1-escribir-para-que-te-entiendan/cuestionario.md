# Unit 1 — Quiz

Key in `../claves/u1-clave-cuestionario.md`. **Part A [AUTO]** · **Part B [HUMAN]**

1. Before writing, the question that shapes the text most is:
   a) How many words should the text be? b) Which sophisticated words can I use? c) Which typeface will I use? d) What does the reader need to do after reading?
2. According to Nielsen's study (2008), on an average web page people read:
   a) About 50%, if the text is clear. b) Almost all of it, if the text is interesting. c) At most 28%; realistically, about 20%. d) Only the headline and the whole first paragraph.
3. The inverted pyramid starts with:
   a) The most important point. b) The background. c) An introduction. d) The general context.
4. "The bug was fixed by the team" in the active voice:
   a) "The team fixed the bug." b) "The bug is fixed." c) "The fixing of the bug." d) "The bug was fixed today."
5. Which is more precise?
   a) "We'll send it soon, no matter what." b) "We'll send it by noon on Friday the 15th." c) "We'll send it as soon as we can." d) "We'll send it shortly, this week."
6. A good heading in web copy:
   a) Is creative even if it doesn't inform. b) Is a single eye-catching word. c) Is in all caps to stand out. d) Sums up what the section says.
7. "It should be noted that" is:
   a) Filler that can usually be cut. b) A passive voice construction. c) A legal term of art. d) Essential for adding emphasis.
8. A technical term in front of a non-technical client:
   a) Goes in as an acronym, like the original. b) Is used without explanation, to sound expert. c) Is avoided or explained the first time. d) Goes in bold so it stands out.
9. When using AI to write, the most important thing is:
   a) Ask it for long, very detailed texts to choose from. b) Give it the reader and purpose, then edit and verify. c) Publish the text as is if it sounds professional. d) Always ask it for very formal, technical language.
10. Reading the text out loud helps you:
    a) Memorize it before presenting it. b) Catch sentences that are hard to read. c) Lengthen it with whatever comes to mind. d) Nothing: it's an old habit.

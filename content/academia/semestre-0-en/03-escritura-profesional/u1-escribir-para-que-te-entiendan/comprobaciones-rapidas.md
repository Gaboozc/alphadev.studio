# Unit 1 — Quick Checks

**Id:** `s0-c3-u1-cr1` to `s0-c3-u1-cr3` · **Format:** quick check (a block inside the guide, `formatos-leccion.md`, section 1) · **Assessment:** [AUTO], ungraded · **Time:** approx. 5 min in total, included in the guide time
**Unit:** Writing to Be Understood · **Key and feedback:** `../claves/u1-clave-comprobaciones.md` (shown on answering; not published with the questions)

> **How they're used.** Each question is inserted in `guia.md` at the end of the section shown. On answering, you immediately see whether you got it right and the explanation; if not, you see a hint with the section to reread and can try again. They don't count toward your grade: they're retrieval practice.

## QC1 · After section 1, Before you write [AUTO]

Before writing, which question changes the text the most?

a) Which technical terms can I use to sound like an expert?
b) How many pages should the document be?
c) What does the reader need to do after reading?

## QC2 · After section 3, The inverted pyramid [AUTO]

With the inverted pyramid, the email starts with…

a) the greeting and the project background.
b) the backstory in chronological order.
c) the conclusion or the request.

## QC3 · After section 4, Clarity at the sentence level [AUTO]

Which of these sentences is in the active voice?

a) "Marta approved the budget."
b) "The budget was approved."
c) "The budget has been approved by the committee."

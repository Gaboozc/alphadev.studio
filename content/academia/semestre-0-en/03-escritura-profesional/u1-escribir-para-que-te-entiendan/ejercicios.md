# Unit 1 — Graded Exercises

Solutions in `../claves/u1-soluciones-ejercicios.md`.

## Level 1 (auto-graded)

**1.1** Active (A) or passive (P)? a) "The client approved the design." b) "The estimate was sent yesterday." c) "The bugs were fixed." d) "We released version 2."

**1.2** Replace the noun phrase with a single verb: a) conduct a review; b) make a payment; c) carry out the installation; d) make an improvement.

**1.3** Which heading informs best? a) "Update" b) "Project news" c) "Launch moves to March 20" d) "Important information"

**1.4** Put these sentences in inverted-pyramid order: (1) Last week the provider changed its API. (2) The store has been unable to take card payments since 3 p.m. yesterday. (3) We're already fixing it; it'll be resolved by 6 p.m. today. (4) That change broke our integration.

## Level 2 (reviewed with a rubric)

**2.1** Edit this text using the checklist from section 6 (submit the annotated original + the final version):
> "Please be advised that, at the platform level, a number of enhancements have been carried out in terms of performance, which have been implemented by our technical team with the objective of optimizing the user experience, and it should be noted that additional updates will be conducted in the near future."

**2.2** Answer the three questions (reader, purpose, action) and write the email for this situation: you need to ask a client to send you the copy for their website, which is two weeks late and is blocking the project.

## Level 3 (reviewed with a rubric)

**3.1** Choose the "Services" or "About" page of a real website. Rewrite it with the structure from section 5 so it can be scanned. Submit the original (screenshot or text), the new version and a 150-word explanation.

**3.2** Ask an AI for an email about a situation of your choice without giving it any context. Then repeat with a prompt that includes the reader, purpose, action and clarity criteria. Compare both results and edit the better one with the checklist from section 6.

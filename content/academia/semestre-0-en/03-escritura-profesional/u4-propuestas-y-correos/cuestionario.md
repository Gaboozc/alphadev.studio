# Unit 4 — Quiz

Key in `../claves/u4-clave-cuestionario.md`. **Part A [AUTO]** · **Part B [HUMAN]**

1. A proposal should start with:
   a) The company's history. b) The client's problem. c) The price and payment terms. d) The portfolio of work.
2. The section that does the most to prevent conflict is:
   a) The scope: what's included and what isn't. b) Both parties' signatures at the end. c) The "Why us" section. d) The greeting and introduction.
3. In the timeline, it's best to include:
   a) Only the project's final delivery date. b) An approximate timeframe: "about 4–6 weeks." c) Milestones with dates and what depends on the client. d) No dates, to avoid committing.
4. In "Why us," the best thing is:
   a) The most similar case, with a verifiable result. b) The whole portfolio, to show range. c) Headcount and years in business. d) The awards and recognition we've won.
5. The executive summary is for:
   a) Filling up the document's first page. b) Informing the decision-maker without reading it all. c) Meeting a legal requirement of the contract. d) Explaining the details to the technical team.
6. Giving context to the price with the client's own data is:
   a) Illegal in most countries. b) Pointless: the client only looks at the total. c) A way of manipulating the client. d) An ethical practice of anchoring with value.
7. A good email subject line:
   a) States the topic and, if relevant, the action and date. b) Is mysterious, to spark curiosity. c) Is a single attention-grabbing word. d) Is in all caps so it isn't missed.
8. Bad news in an email should go:
   a) It isn't written: it's delivered by phone. b) At the end, after the good news. c) In an attachment, so as not to alarm. d) In the first line, along with options.
9. A sales email should have:
   a) Several calls to action, just in case. b) None, so as not to seem pushy. c) One clear call to action. d) Just a link to the website, with no text.
10. A good follow-up:
    a) Is sent every day until there's a reply. b) Tactfully scolds the lack of reply. c) Adds something new and makes replying easy. d) Repeats the previous email as a reminder.

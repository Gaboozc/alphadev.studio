# Unit 4 — Graded Exercises

Solutions in `../claves/u4-soluciones-ejercicios.md`.

## Level 1 (auto-graded)

**1.1** Put the parts of the proposal in order: (a) Investment (b) Why us (c) The problem (d) Next steps (e) Timeline (f) What's included and what isn't (g) The solution

**1.2** Which subject line is best? a) "Follow-up" b) "Hi" c) "Website proposal: can we review it Thursday at 11?" d) "IMPORTANT!!!"

**1.3** Which pricing practice is unethical? a) Offering three real packages b) Giving context with the data the client provided c) Comparing with "what a freelancer would charge" with no source d) Stating recurring costs

**1.4** Calculation: the recommended package is $4,500, and in your state the service carries 8% sales tax. What's the total? If the client pays a 50% deposit on the total, how much is the deposit?

## Level 2 (reviewed with a rubric)

**2.1** Rewrite this proposal opening: "Dear Valued Client, We are Pixel Studio, an agency founded in 2015 with over 200 projects, specializing in innovative digital solutions."
(Client details: a dental practice with 3 locations; they take appointments only by phone; they miss calls over lunch.)

**2.2** Write the "What's included and what isn't" section and the timeline for the dental practice (a website with online appointment booking).

**2.3** Write an email with bad news: the design will be 5 days late because the client sent a low-resolution logo and it had to be requested again. No blame, with options.

## Level 3 (reviewed with a rubric)

**3.1** Write a sales email for prospects who know your brand but haven't bought: 3 subject line + preview text variations for an A/B test, the full email (opening, PAS body, real social proof or proof labeled as fictional, CTA), a screenshot of how it looks on mobile (approx. 375 px) and a 150-word analysis of the persuasion principles used and why. *(The basis of Part 4 of the final project; replaces the original intermediate project "Complete sales email.")*

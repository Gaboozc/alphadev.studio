# Unit 4 — Reflection (reviewed with a rubric)

## Prompt (300–500 words)
Look through your inbox (or at a proposal you've received or sent).
1. Choose the best and the worst professional email you've received recently. What makes them that way, according to section 3?
2. Which of your own email habits do you now recognize as problematic?
3. If you've negotiated prices (as a client or a provider), do you recognize any use of anchoring? Was it ethical?

## Rubric (12 points)
| Criterion | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Analysis of emails | Both analyzed with the criteria from section 3, with quotes | One analyzed well | Superficial | Missing |
| Self-critique | Specific habits with an example | General | Vague | Missing |
| Price and ethics | A specific case with an argued ethical judgment | A case without judgment | A vague mention | Missing |
| Clarity of their own text | Applies U1 exemplarily | Clear with minor lapses | Confusing | Illegible |

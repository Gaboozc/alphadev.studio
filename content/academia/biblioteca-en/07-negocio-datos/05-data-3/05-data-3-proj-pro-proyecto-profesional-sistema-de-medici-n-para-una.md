## Project brief

Design and implement the complete measurement system for a multi-channel digital campaign.

## Deliverables
- Measurement framework: business goal → KPIs → metrics → data sources for each channel
- GA4 setup: events configured (minimum 5 custom events relevant to the campaign), Goals defined
- Attribution: chosen attribution model with justification for why that model and not another for this specific case
- Campaign dashboard in Looker Studio: all channels in one place, comparison vs. prior periods
- Reporting plan: cadence, format and audience for each report (weekly operational vs. monthly executive)
- QA protocol: how you verify tracking is capturing data accurately

## Rubric
- KPIs connect directly to the business goal, not vanity metrics
- GA4 setup is implemented and verified (not just designed)
- Chosen attribution reflects that business's actual customer journey
- Dashboard is interpretable by someone who didn't configure the system
- QA protocol can catch tracking issues in under 30 minutes

## Tip

The most expensive mistake in measurement is finding out tracking was broken after the campaign ended. Put QA in place from day 1.

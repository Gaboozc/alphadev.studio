## Project brief

Write the complete copy for a landing page for a mid-to-high-ticket product or service ($200+). Every block, from hero to footer.

## Deliverables
- Hero: main headline, subheadline and primary + secondary CTA
- Problem block: pain point in the customer's language (their words, not yours)
- Solution block: outcome-driven benefits, not features
- 3 testimonials with name, context and specific measurable result
- Objections block: the 3 most common doubts answered with empathy
- Final CTA: urgency or guarantee + repeat of the CTA
- Meta description and OG title for SEO and social

## Rubric
- Every block has a clear goal in the conversion flow
- Language is the customer's, not the seller's — no internal jargon
- Testimonials have specific, measurable results
- The price objection is answered even if no price is visible
- Tone is consistent from start to finish

## Tip

Before writing a single word, write down what the prospect is thinking right before landing on the page. That thought is your headline.

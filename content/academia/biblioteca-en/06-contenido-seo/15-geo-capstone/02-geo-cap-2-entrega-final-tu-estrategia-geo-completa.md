## Project brief

Deliver a complete GEO plan with partial implementation for a real brand. The goal is to show you can audit, strategize, create GEO-optimized content and measure results.

## Deliverables
- GEO audit report (documented baseline)
- 6-month strategy with AI Mention Rate goals
- Minimum 3 pieces of content published and optimized for GEO
- Schema markup implemented on at least 2 pages
- Metrics dashboard with first month of real data

## Rubric
- Audit with real data and clear methodology
- Strategy tied directly to identified gaps
- Content applying GEO principles (structure, data, E-E-A-T)
- Real measurement, even if only 1–2 queries improved

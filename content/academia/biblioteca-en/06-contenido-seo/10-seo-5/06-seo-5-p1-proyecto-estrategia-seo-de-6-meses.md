## Project brief

Develop a complete 6-month SEO strategy for a real website. Include thorough keyword research, content plan, link-building strategy and traffic projection with documented assumptions.

## Deliverables
- Keyword research with at least 50 prioritized keywords
- Month-by-month content map (6 months)
- Link-building plan with 10 identified opportunities
- Traffic projection with assumptions model
- KPIs and monthly reporting methodology

## Rubric
- Keyword research with real data (Search Volume, KD)
- Content aligned to search intent
- Viable, non-manipulative link building
- Projection with realistic, documented assumptions

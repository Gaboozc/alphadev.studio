## Project brief

Design a complete growth strategy for an existing digital product. Identify the core viral loop and propose 3 growth experiments with hypotheses, metrics and success/failure criteria. Present the plan as if pitching to investors.

## Deliverables
- Analysis of the product's current viral loop
- 3 experiments in RICE format (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort)
- Implementation roadmap by 2-week sprint
- Metrics dashboard with 90-day targets

## Rubric
- Viral loop accurately identified and documented
- Experiments with falsifiable hypotheses
- RICE prioritization justified
- Investor-grade presentation

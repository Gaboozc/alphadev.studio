## The brief

Pick a real business — AlphaDev Studios, a relative's business, a friend or a prospective client — and run a complete audit of its digital presence. The result is a PDF or Notion report demonstrating your strategic analysis skills. This kind of deliverable is exact what clients pay for in consulting.

## Why this project matters

A digital presence audit is the first service many agencies offer — often free as a lead magnet. Learning to do it well opens the door to bigger, paid projects.

## The step-by-step process

**Step 1 — Pick the business**
If it's someone else's business, ask permission. Explain it's a training project and you're giving them the results for free. Almost everyone says yes.

**Step 2 — Audit the website**
- Speed: run the site through PageSpeed Insights — record mobile and desktop scores
- Basic SEO: does it have a meta title, meta description, clear H1 on the home page?
- Message clarity: can you understand the business's value in 5 seconds?
- CTA: is there a clear call to action visible without scrolling?
- Mobile: does it display properly on a phone screen?

**Step 3 — Audit social media**
For each active channel, record:
- Date of the last post and average frequency
- Dominant content format (video / image / text)
- Estimated engagement rate ((likes + comments) / followers × 100)
- Profile completeness: bio, photo, links, highlights

**Step 4 — Audit local search (if applicable)**
- Does it have a verified Google Business Profile?
- Does it have reviews? Does it reply to them?
- Are the photos up to date?
- Are hours and contact info accurate?

**Step 5 — Put the report together**
1. Executive summary (3 lines: what you found overall)
2. Website: strengths + opportunities for improvement
3. Social media: channel-by-channel analysis
4. Local search (if applicable)
5. Priority recommendations: 5 actions ranked by impact
6. Quick wins: what they can do this week at zero cost and with no designer

## Tasks
- Pick the business to audit and ask permission if it's a third party
- Complete the website analysis with PageSpeed Insights and a point-by-point manual review
- Audit at least 2 social channels with real metrics recorded
- Put together the complete report with 5 recommendations prioritized by impact
- Deliver the report to the business and ask for feedback — that conversation is the most valuable learning
- Publish an anonymized summary of the project on LinkedIn (with permission)

## Tip

The best audits aren't the ones that find the most problems — they're the ones that prioritize best. A client can't implement 20 changes at once. The 3 quick wins they can do in 48 hours without hiring anyone are what build instant trust and turn a free audit into an implementation contract.

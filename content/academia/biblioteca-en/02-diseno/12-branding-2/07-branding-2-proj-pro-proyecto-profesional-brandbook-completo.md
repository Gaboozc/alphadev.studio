## Project brief

Design the complete visual identity system for the brand you've developed. The deliverable is the brand book you would hand over to a real client.

## Deliverables
- Logo: primary, compact, monochrome and dark-background versions
- Color system: full palette with name, hex/RGB/CMYK and usage guide
- Typography system: typeface(s), full scale and allowed combinations
- Graphic elements: patterns or supporting iconography
- Minimum 4 brand applications on real mockups
- Usage guide: what NOT to do (clear space, forbidden colors, disallowed typefaces)
- File: organized, navigable Figma file or PDF of at least 20 pages

## Rubric
- The logo works across all sizes and contexts
- The palette meets WCAG AA contrast
- Applications are consistent with one another
- The guide lets someone apply the identity without consulting the designer

## Tip

The most valuable brand book is the clearest one, not the prettiest one. A client who can apply the identity without calling you is the goal.

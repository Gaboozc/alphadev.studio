# Project: Test a Real Product and Deliver Your Report

## Project brief

Apply the full process from this module to a real product: yours, a client's, or a public product you use often. The goal isn't to find problems for the sake of finding them — it's to produce a report a design team can use to decide what to fix first.

## Deliverables
- Testing plan: 4–5 concrete tasks, the chosen method (unmoderated remote, moderated remote or in-person) and user recruitment criteria
- Minimum 3 completed sessions (recorded with permission, or with detailed notes if recording wasn't possible)
- Quantitative metrics: completion rate per task and SUS score
- Findings report: minimum 5 findings, each with observation + evidence (clip or verbatim quote) + recommendation
- Impact/effort prioritization matrix with the findings plotted
- 10-minute presentation (video or live) of the report to someone who didn't take part in testing

## Rubric
- The testing plan has specific, measurable tasks, not generic ones ("browse the site")
- Findings are backed by real evidence, not the designer's opinions
- The prioritization matrix reflects real measured impact, not just intuition
- The report is understandable to someone who didn't watch the sessions

## Tip

The most common mistake in this project is testing your own product too leniently — it's hard to be objective about your own design. If you can, get someone from outside to review your findings report before submitting, looking for biases that slipped in.
